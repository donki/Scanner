## Description

```
sOC Scanner scans with a single button (or Enter) using any scanner Windows recognises or a camera, one sheet at a time or in a series, to PDF, JPG, PNG, TIFF or BMP.

ANY SCANNER OR CAMERA
• USB and network scanners and all-in-one printers, flatbed or document feeder.
• A camera works too: your laptop camera, a webcam or a document camera, with live preview.

QUALITY IN ONE CLICK
• Black-and-white text, grey document, colour document or photo; or custom: resolution, colour, page size, brightness, contrast and JPEG quality, with the size of each page shown up front.
• Anything the device can't do is adjusted automatically and explained.

ONE BY ONE OR IN A SERIES
• One by one: scan, rotate, reorder or remove pages and save them as a multi-page PDF or TIFF, or as JPG, PNG or BMP.
• Series: each page saves itself as soon as it's scanned, with a sequential name (Invoice_0007.pdf). It never overwrites a file.
• When done: open, show in folder or copy the file to paste into an email or chat.

Errors are explained (out of paper, jam, lid open, camera permission, disk full…) with what to do. No internet connection, nothing uploaded. No account, no ads, no trackers. Free software under the MIT licence. In English and Spanish, light and dark mode.
```
